Come Quando Fuori Piove

The classic trick-taking, bid-calling card game — from the living room to the beach, now on your phone.

Before each hand you declare how many tricks you'll win. Call it exactly right and you score; miss it, even by one, and you lose a point. Play solo against bots, pass the phone around the table, or take it online with Game Center.
